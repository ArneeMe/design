## Penguin

Three ways to draw the penguin without pixels, next to today's pixel penguin. Open `penguin/index.html` to compare them at full size, 48 and 24 pixels, on the dark plate and on paper.

- A, flat and geometric: solid shapes, nebb orange beak and feet.
- B, one line: a 2px outline with square ends, like the icons would be.
- C, linocut: black and white only, uneven edges and carved marks. Works on e-ink as it is.

All three keep the pixel penguin's build: wide and low, a two-lobed white face and slanted eyes.

When one is chosen, it moves into `themes/waddle/` and gets generated like the pixel penguin. The icons follow the same style.
